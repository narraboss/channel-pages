Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The Price Peel publishing tool ("the app") is a private application used only by the owner of the Price Peel YouTube channel to publish and manage videos on that channel. It is not available to the public.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube account access for the owner's own channel, granted through Google OAuth: uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions and thumbnails, managing playlists, and reading the channel's own comments and analytics.
  • No data from other YouTube users is collected, except public comments on the channel's own videos, which are read only to understand viewer questions.

How data is used

Data is used solely to publish the channel's videos and to measure their performance (views, watch time, retention) so the channel can improve its content. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and analytics are stored on the owner's private servers, are not shared, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
