Proof of Wow

Proof of Wow is a YouTube channel of short science explainers that check whether amazing claims are actually true. Every claim is checked against peer-reviewed papers and official science agencies, and the evidence is shown on screen.

This site belongs to the Proof of Wow publishing tool, a private application the channel owner uses to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
