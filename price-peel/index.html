Price Peel

Price Peel is a YouTube channel of short animated explainers that peel back what is inside everyday prices: where the money for a coffee, a gallon of milk or a bottle of insulin actually goes. Every number comes from public, cited sources.

This site belongs to the Price Peel publishing tool, a private application the channel owner uses to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
